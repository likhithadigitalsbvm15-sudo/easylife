What Is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps shoppers quickly determine the final price of an item after applying percentage discounts during festive sales, clearance offers, or coupon promotions. EasyLife's Discount Calculator also supports stacked (double) discounts, such as "20% Off + Extra 10% Off", showing your exact total savings.

How Are Discounts Calculated?

1. Single Discount Formula:

Discount Amount = (Original Price × Discount%) / 100  |  Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

2. Double / Stacked Discount Formula:

Price After 1st Discount = Original Price − First Discount  |  Final Price = Price After 1st Discount − Extra Discount

How to Use This Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the item's Original Price (MRP) in rupees.
  2. Enter the main Discount (%) offered on the product.
  3. If you have an additional bank or coupon discount, enter it in Extra Discount (%) (or leave it blank).
  4. Click Calculate Discount to see the Final Payable Price and Total Savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is "20% + 10% off" the same as 30% off?

No. In stacked discounts, the extra 10% is applied to the already discounted price (not the original MRP), which equals an effective total discount of 28%.

2. Can I use this tool for wholesale or invoice discounts?

Yes. Shopkeepers and buyers can calculate trade discounts on any price amount accurately.

3. Is this Discount Calculator free to use?

Yes, it is 100% free and works instantly in your browser on mobile phones and computers.
