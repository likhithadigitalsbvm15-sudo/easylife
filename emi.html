What Is an EMI Calculator?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount a borrower pays to a bank or lender every month until the loan is fully repaid. Each monthly payment covers both a portion of the principal loan amount and the interest charged on the remaining balance.

EasyLife's EMI Calculator helps you estimate your monthly commitment for Home Loans, Car Loans, Personal Loans, or Education Loans in seconds.

How Is Loan EMI Calculated?

Banks and financial institutions use the standard reducing-balance EMI formula:

EMI = [P × R × (1 + R)^N] / [(1 + R)^N − 1]
  • P: Principal loan amount borrowed
  • R: Monthly interest rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • N: Total loan tenure in months

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I calculate EMI for Home, Car, and Personal loans?

Yes. The same standard reducing-balance formula applies to home loans, vehicle loans, and personal loans.

2. How does loan tenure affect my EMI?

A longer tenure reduces your monthly EMI amount, but increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan. A shorter tenure increases monthly EMI while saving significantly on total interest.

3. Does this calculator include bank processing fees?

No. This tool calculates pure principal and interest repayments. Check with your lender for processing fees, insurance, or GST charges.

4. Is my financial data saved anywhere?

No. All calculations happen instantly inside your browser and nothing is stored or transmitted.
