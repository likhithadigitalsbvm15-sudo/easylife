What Is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage represents a number or ratio expressed as a fraction of 100. EasyLife's Percentage Calculator provides three essential percentage tools on a single page—helping students calculate exam percentages, shoppers check discount amounts, and businesses measure growth or price changes.

Percentage Formulas

1. Finding X% of a number Y:

Result = (X / 100) × Y

2. Finding what percentage X is of Y (Marks Percentage):

Percentage (%) = (X / Y) × 100

3. Finding Percentage Increase or Decrease:

Percentage Change (%) = [(Final Value − Initial Value) / Initial Value] × 100
