Why Use a Strong Password Generator?

Using simple or repeated passwords like birthdays, phone numbers, or common words makes your email, banking, and social media accounts vulnerable to automated attacks. EasyLife's Password Generator uses your browser's built-in cryptographic randomizer (window.crypto) to create unpredictable, high-entropy passwords that are virtually impossible to guess.

Best Practices for Online Account Security

  • Use at least 12–16 characters: Longer passwords dramatically increase security against brute-force attacks.
  • Mix character types: Combine uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols.
  • Never reuse passwords: Create a unique password for every important account (email, banking, social media).
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Add an extra layer of protection whenever available.
