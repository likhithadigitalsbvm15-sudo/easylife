What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is a quick online utility that finds the exact time interval between your date of birth and today (or any chosen target date). It automatically accounts for varying month lengths (28, 30, or 31 days) and leap years to give you an accurate breakdown in years, months, and days.

How to Use This Age Calculator

  1. Select your Date of Birth from the calendar picker.
  2. Keep Age at the Date of set to today, or pick a specific cutoff date required for an application form.
  3. Click Calculate Age to view your exact age, total months, total weeks, total days, and next birthday countdown.

Common Uses of an Age Calculator

  • Job & Exam Applications: Verify age eligibility on specific government or competitive exam cutoff dates.
  • School & College Admissions: Check exact child age requirements for kindergarten or grade admissions.
  • Personal Milestones: Find out what day of the week you were born on and how many days until your next birthday.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does this age calculator handle leap years accurately?

Yes. It uses exact calendar month lengths and accounts for February 29 in leap years.

2. Can I check my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Simply change the Age at the Date of field to any date after your birth date.

3. Is my date of birth stored or shared?

No. All date calculations happen locally in your browser and no personal information is saved.
