What Is a GST Calculator?

A Goods and Services Tax (GST) calculator helps businesses, shopkeepers, freelancers, and consumers quickly calculate the tax component on goods and services. EasyLife's GST Calculator supports both adding GST to a base price (GST Exclusive) and extracting GST from a final MRP price (GST Inclusive).

GST Calculation Formulas

1. To Add GST (Exclusive):

GST Amount = (Original Cost × GST%) / 100  |  Net Price = Original Cost + GST Amount

2. To Remove GST (Inclusive):

Original Cost = Total Amount / (1 + GST% / 100)  |  GST Amount = Total Amount − Original Cost

How to Use This GST Calculator

  1. Enter the Amount in rupees.
  2. Select the applicable GST Slab Rate (3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%).
  3. Choose Add GST if your amount excludes tax, or Remove GST if tax is already included.
  4. Click Calculate GST to view the Net Amount, CGST, SGST, IGST, and Total Amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are CGST, SGST, and IGST?

For intra-state sales (within the same state), GST is split equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For inter-state sales, the full rate is charged as Integrated GST (IGST).

2. How do I find the base price from a GST-inclusive bill?

Enter the final bill amount, choose the GST slab rate, and select Remove GST (- GST Inclusive) from the Calculation Type dropdown.

3. Is this GST calculator free to use?

Yes, it is completely free and calculates everything instantly in your browser.
