What Is an Interest Calculator?

An interest calculator helps you find out how much extra money you earn on savings or pay on a borrowed amount over a specific period. EasyLife's Interest Calculator supports both Simple Interest and Compound Interest, with flexible time inputs in years, months, or days.

Interest Formulas Used

1. Simple Interest Formula:

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100

2. Compound Interest Formula (Compounded Annually):

Total Amount (A) = P × (1 + R / 100)^T  |  CI = A − P
  • P: Principal amount
  • R: Annual rate of interest (%)
  • T: Time period in years

How to Use This Interest Calculator

  1. Choose Simple Interest or Compound Interest.
  2. Enter the Principal Amount in rupees.
  3. Enter the annual Interest Rate (%).
  4. Enter the Time Period and select Years, Months, or Days.
  5. Click Calculate Interest to see the total interest and final amount immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between Simple Interest and Compound Interest?

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal amount. Compound interest is calculated on the principal plus any accumulated interest from previous years.

2. Can I calculate interest for months or days?

Yes. Select Months (divided by 12) or Days (divided by 365) next to the time period input.

3. Is this tool free to use?

Yes, EasyLife's Interest Calculator is 100% free and runs directly in your browser without storing any data.
