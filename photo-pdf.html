How to Convert Photos to PDF

  1. Select Add Photos.
  2. Choose one or more images from your phone or computer.
  3. Check the image previews and remove any unwanted photo.
  4. Select A4 or Letter for the PDF page size.
  5. Press Create PDF and wait for the download.
  6. Open the downloaded PDF to check each page.

When Is Photo to PDF Useful?

Combining photos into a PDF can make it easier to share multiple pages in a single file. For example, you might combine pictures of handwritten notes, classwork, receipts or non-sensitive forms.

Each selected image is placed on a separate PDF page. The tool does not automatically recognize text or make the PDF searchable. Check the requirements of the website or office receiving your file before submitting it.

Tips for Better Results

  • Take photos in good light and keep the camera steady.
  • Make sure the important text is fully visible in each image.
  • Choose photos in the order you want them to appear in the PDF.
  • Use A4 when a form or office specifically requests A4 pages.
  • Large images may take longer to process and create a larger PDF.
  • Open the downloaded file and verify that all pages are present.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I convert several photos into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple images. The tool places each image on a separate page in the same PDF.

2. Which page sizes are available?

This page offers A4 and Letter page sizes. Choose the one required by the person or organization receiving your PDF.

3. Why is my PDF taking a long time to download?

Large photos or many images can take longer to process. Try using smaller image files if your device runs out of memory or the browser becomes unresponsive.

4. Does this tool convert images into searchable text?

No. It places the selected images into a PDF. It does not include optical character recognition (OCR).

5. Can I use this on a mobile phone?

Yes, you can select photos from a supported mobile browser. The exact file picker and download behavior depend on your device.

6. Are the selected photos sent to an EasyLife server?

The tool's JavaScript creates the PDF in your browser and contains no request that uploads the selected images to an EasyLife server. The page does load external resources, including the PDF library and advertising script. For sensitive documents, use a trusted device and review the site's Privacy Policy.
