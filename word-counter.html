What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an essential writing tool that calculates the exact number of words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in any text. Whether you are writing a school essay, blog article, YouTube description, or social media post with strict character limits, EasyLife's Word Counter gives instant feedback as you type.

How to Use This Word Counter

  1. Click inside the text box above and start typing, or paste copied text from another document.
  2. Watch the live counters update automatically for Words, Characters, Characters (No Spaces), Sentences, Paragraphs, and Estimated Reading Time.
  3. Use the quick action buttons below the box to convert text to UPPERCASE or lowercase, copy your text, or clear the editor.

Common Character & Word Limits

  • Meta Description (SEO): 150 – 160 characters
  • X (Twitter) Post: Up to 280 characters
  • Instagram Caption: Up to 2,200 characters
  • YouTube Video Title: Up to 100 characters (70 recommended)
  • Standard Blog Post: 800 – 1,500+ words

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How is estimated reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated based on an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute.

2. Does this tool save or upload the text I paste?

No. Everything runs locally inside your web browser—your essays, articles, and notes are never sent to any server.

3. Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes. It works smoothly on Android, iPhone, tablets, and desktop computers.
