How to Use the Document Scanner

This tool helps you prepare a clean document image directly in your browser by selecting a photo, rotating it, cropping unwanted background edges, applying scan filters, and downloading the final JPEG.

  1. Select a document: Click Scan / Upload Document and choose an image.
  2. Rotate if needed: Click Rotate 90° if your photo is sideways or upside down.
  3. Crop the edges: Click Crop Document, drag over the paper edges, and click Apply Crop.
  4. Apply a scan filter: Choose Black & White Scan or Magic Color for clearer text.
  5. Download: Click Download Scanned JPG to save the prepared document.

Tips for Clear Document Scans

  • Place the paper on a flat surface with good natural lighting.
  • Keep the camera parallel to the document to avoid angled distortion.
  • Avoid shadows, glare, and fingers covering the text.
  • Leave a small margin around important text, signatures, and stamps when cropping.
  • Open the downloaded image to verify readability before sharing or printing.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which files can I select?

You can select standard image files such as JPEG, PNG, and WebP from your phone or computer.

2. Can I crop and scan an image on a mobile phone?

Yes. Open the crop tool and drag your finger over the image to select the exact document borders.

3. What does the Black & White Scan filter do?

It removes color shadows and increases contrast so printed or handwritten text looks crisp like a physical scanner.

4. Does this tool create a PDF?

It downloads the scanned document as a high-quality JPEG image. You can use our Photo to PDF tool if you need a PDF file.

5. Are my documents uploaded to a server?

No. All cropping, rotation, and filtering happen locally inside your web browser for complete privacy.
