Privacy Policy

EasyLife ❤️ — How We Handle Data and Protect Your Privacy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to EasyLife (https://easylifetoolbox.com/). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website and use our free online PDF, image, calculator, and utility tools.

1. Client-Side File Processing & User Files

Most tools on EasyLife—including Photo to PDF, PDF to Image, PDF Compress, Document Scanner, Photo Resizer, Image Converter, Calculators, Word Counter, and Password Generator—run directly inside your web browser using local HTML5 and JavaScript technologies.

  • No File Storage: Images, PDFs, and documents you select are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to or stored on EasyLife servers.
  • Sensitive Documents: We do not require user accounts, registration, or personal details to use our tools. However, we advise users to exercise caution when handling confidential documents on any public or shared device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, or phone number) unless you voluntarily provide it when contacting us via our Contact Us page.

Like most websites, standard non-personal technical data—such as browser type, device type, operating system, referring pages, and general usage statistics—may be collected automatically through analytics and advertising services to improve website performance.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

EasyLife uses cookies and local browser storage to remember basic preferences, support Progressive Web App (PWA) caching, analyze website traffic, and serve relevant advertisements. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings at any time.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

EasyLife uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display ads and keep our tools free for everyone.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to EasyLife and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn more about how Google uses data at Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

5. Google Analytics & Third-Party APIs

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate visitor traffic and improve our tools. Additionally, specific tools (such as the Language Translator or Voice-to-Text features) rely on browser speech APIs or third-party translation endpoints to return results; these services operate under their respective privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

EasyLife does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our contact channels, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. Please note that we do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to our tools or changes in legal and advertising requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please visit our Contact Us page.
