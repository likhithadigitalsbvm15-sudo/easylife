What Is a Voice to Text Converter?

A Voice to Text (Speech-to-Text) converter uses your device's microphone and browser speech recognition technology to transcribe spoken words into digital text in real time. EasyLife's Voice Typing tool supports Telugu, English, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam without requiring any software installation.

How to Use Voice Typing Online

  1. Choose your preferred speaking language from the dropdown menu.
  2. Click Start Speaking and click Allow if your browser asks for microphone permission.
  3. Speak clearly into your microphone; your words will appear in the text box automatically.
  4. Click Stop when you are finished speaking.
  5. Edit the text if needed, then click Copy Text or Download TXT to save your notes.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which browsers support Voice to Text?

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Safari on desktop and Android devices provide the best support for Web Speech Recognition.

2. Can I type in Telugu or Hindi using my voice?

Yes. Select తెలుగు (Telugu) or हिंदी (Hindi) from the language selector before clicking Start Speaking.

3. Is my voice recorded or stored by EasyLife?

No. EasyLife does not record or store your audio or transcribed text on any server.
