What Is Photo Resizing?

Photo resizing changes the pixel width and height of an image. It helps prepare pictures for websites, government application forms, social media, email, or printing. The best dimensions depend on where you plan to use the photo.

This tool lets you select a photo, enter custom dimensions or pick a preset, preview the result with its new file size, and download a JPG or PNG copy. Your original file is never overwritten.

How to Resize a Photo

  1. Select a photo from your device.
  2. Check the original dimensions and file size shown above the input fields.
  3. Enter your required width and height in pixels, or click a Quick Preset.
  4. Choose JPG for smaller file sizes or PNG to preserve transparent backgrounds.
  5. Click Resize Photo to generate a preview.
  6. Click Download Resized Photo to save your new image.

Why Keep the Original Proportions?

Every photo has a width-to-height aspect ratio. If the width and height are changed independently, faces or objects can look stretched or squashed. Keeping the original proportions enabled calculates the matching dimension automatically.

For example, if a photo is 1200 × 800 pixels and you change its width to 600 pixels with proportions enabled, its height automatically becomes 400 pixels.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does px mean?

Px means pixels, the tiny dots that make up a digital image.

Will the original photo be deleted?

No. This tool creates a new downloadable image in your browser and never modifies your original file.

Why does my resized photo look stretched?

The new width and height may have a different ratio from the original. Keep “Keep original photo proportions” checked to avoid stretching.

How do I keep a transparent background?

Select PNG (Keep Transparency) under the Output Format dropdown before resizing.

How can I reduce the KB file size for online forms?

Reduce the pixel width/height (such as clicking 50% Smaller) and choose JPG with Medium or Low quality.
